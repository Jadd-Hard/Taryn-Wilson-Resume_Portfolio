## 🔬 SECTION 2: REPAIR, SHANK REBUILDING & SIZING DEMONSTRATIONS

One of the most critical daily functions of a commercial workshop is the execution of clean, structurally sound repairs. The projects below demonstrate advanced metal control and heat management.

### **4. Advanced Aquamarine Ring Sizing using Heat-Sink Protective Clay**
*   **The Challenge:** Resizing a gold/platinum ring set with a heat-sensitive light blue Aquamarine center stone without unsetting the gem.
*   **Techniques:** 
    1. Shank is cut cleanly at the base.
    2. The top of the ring (including the sensitive aquamarine gem and side stones) is fully encased in a specialized **green heat-protection compound** (heat-sink clay).
    3. Precision micro-torch heating is applied specifically to the bottom shank seam to flow the solder while keeping the gemstone safely at room temperature.
    4. Solder is flowed, and the shank is perfectly joined and rounded.

![Aquamarine Ring Sizing with Heat-Sink Clay](Capture8985.PNG)

*Figure 4: Complete four-panel bench sequence showing cutting, packing in protective compound, soldering under the torch, and the final polished aquamarine ring.*
